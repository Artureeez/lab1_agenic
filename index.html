Typing Defence

Enemies are coming for your base. Type the word on an enemy to shoot it down.

  • Type the first letter of a word to lock on.
  • Finish the word to destroy the enemy.
  • Typos break your combo. Clean waves repair the base.
  • Every 5th wave brings a boss with a whole sentence to type.
  • Shoot glowing power-ups: ❤ repair, ❄ slow-mo, ★ cheat mode (rare).
